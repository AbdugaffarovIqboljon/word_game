## Full description (≤4000)
So'z Jangi is the **daily word game for the Uzbek language**. Every day brings a
fresh 5-letter word — you get 6 tries to guess it!

It's a modern, **Uzbek Wordle**-style puzzle. After each guess the tile colors
guide you:
• 🟩 green — the letter is in the right spot;
• 🟨 yellow — the letter is in the word, but elsewhere;
• ⬛️ gray — the letter isn't in the word.

The game fully supports the Uzbek Latin alphabet, including the compound letters
**oʻ, gʻ, sh, ch, ng** — each counts as a single tile, exactly like the keyboard.

### Why So'z Jangi?
• **Word of the day** — one new puzzle daily; the whole country solves the same
  word. Share your result with friends on Telegram!
• **Streaks** — play every day and grow your fire streak. Missed a day? A freeze
  saves your streak.
• **Practice mode** — play as much as you like across three tiers: easy, medium,
  hard.
• **Hints** — reveal a letter or clear the keyboard when you're stuck.
• **Statistics** — wins, average guesses, and your best streak.
• **Themes** — unlock new tile designs with coins and gems.
• **Fully offline** — play without an internet connection.

Words are sourced from an Uzbek Wikipedia frequency list and the MUNIS Latin
spelling dictionary — only real, commonly used words.

Sharpen your mind daily, grow your Uzbek vocabulary, and compete with friends.
**So'z Jangi** — one word a day, one win a day!
