# Privacy Policy — So'z Jangi

_Last updated: 2026-07-11 · Applies to the So'z Jangi mobile app (Android & iOS)._

## Summary
So'z Jangi has **no user accounts and no login**. We do not ask for your name,
email, phone number, or address. Your game progress lives **on your device**. We
use a few standard third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, ads, and
in-app purchases, described below.

## Information we process

### 1. Data stored only on your device
Game progress, streaks, statistics, settings, wallet (coins/gems), owned items,
and the remove-ads flag are stored locally on your device (via the OS key–value
store) and are **not transmitted to us**. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

### 2. Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics
We collect anonymous, aggregated usage events (e.g. session start, game
start/finish, screens and features used) to understand how the game is played
and improve it. This includes app instance identifiers and general device/app
information. It is **not** used to identify you personally.

### 3. Crash reporting — Google Firebase Crashlytics
If the app crashes, we receive a diagnostic report (stack trace, device model, OS
version, app state) so we can fix bugs.

### 4. Remote configuration — Google Firebase Remote Config
We fetch game-balance settings (rewards, ad frequency) from a server. No personal
data is sent.

### 5. Advertising — Google AdMob (and its partners)
We show rewarded and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its
advertising partners may use your device's **advertising identifier** and related
data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted.
- On first launch we show a **consent screen (Google UMP)**. On iOS we also
  request **App Tracking Transparency** permission. Your choices control whether
  personalized advertising is used.
- Purchasing **remove-ads** stops interstitial ads.
- Google's practices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### 6. In-app purchases — Apple App Store / Google Play
Purchases (remove-ads, gems, starter pack, hint pack) are processed by Apple or
Google. **We never receive or store your payment card details.** We store only
whether an item was purchased, to deliver it.

### 7. Notifications
With your permission, we schedule a local daily reminder about your streak. It is
generated **on your device** — no push server, no FCM. You can turn it off in
Settings at any time.

## Children
The game is family-friendly and contains no accounts or user-generated content.
It is not directed at children under 13; where required, ad personalization is
disabled without consent.

## Data sharing
We share data only with the service providers above (Google/Apple) to operate the
described features. We do not sell your data.

## Your choices
- Change ad/tracking consent in your device settings (and via the in-app UMP/ATT
  prompts).
- Turn off notifications in Settings.
- Reset all local data by uninstalling the app.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change. Material changes
will be announced in the app or store listing.
